## What changes

The current backdrop is a blue-dominant gradient with a bright diagonal sweep and
heavy grain. The new one is near-black with a cool cast:

- **Ground:** `#050608`, with the design's radial lift
  `radial-gradient(120% 100% at 50% 0%, #0d0f16, #050608 60%)` where the design
  uses it.
- **Diagonal sweep:** the design keeps a diagonal light band across the hero, but
  far dimmer and cooler than ours — a soft `n800`/`n700` wash, not a white streak.
  Read its angle and extent from the design and match them. Keep our existing
  `sweep_light.dart` animation; drop its amplitude.
- **Dashes:** the design scatters short angled dashes (`n700`-ish, 1–2px) across
  the hero. We already draw something like these — retint and re-space them to the
  design rather than adding a new painter.
- **Grain:** `assets/textures/bg_grain_a.png` / `bg_grain_b.png` stay, but at much
  lower opacity over a near-black ground. Tune by eye against the reference PNGs;
  grain should be felt, not seen.
- **`poster_tokens.dart`** is this prompt's to rewrite. Every colour in it that
  still reads blue moves onto `lib/theme/nocturne.dart`. Where a poster token has
  no Nocturne equivalent, keep the token but rederive its value from the Nocturne
  ramp — do not leave an orphan hex.

## Layer order (get this right)

ground → radial lift → sweep light → dashes → grain → **device stage** →
callout cards. The grain sits *under* the device, not over it; the design's mockup
is crisp.

## Performance

The repo has prior art on this: `claude/P12_animation_frame_budget.md`,
`P12_sweep_light_jank.md`, `P12B_morph_jank_round2.md`,
`P12C_orb_and_callout_jumps.md` and `ANIMATION_PERF.md` in the project docs.
**Read them before touching the sweep animation.** Whatever budget they establish
still applies; a dimmer sweep must not become a more expensive one. If you add a
repaint boundary or remove one, say why in the report.

## Do not

- Do not add a new texture asset.
- Do not introduce a `BackdropFilter` blur over the whole hero — it is the classic
  way to tank web frame rate here.
- Do not touch the device frames (sk9) or the cards (sk8).
