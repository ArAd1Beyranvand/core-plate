## The one non-negotiable

**The plates stay ours.** The design's plates are drawn wrong — wrong proportions,
wrong country blocks, wrong colours. Every plate on this page is rendered by our
country packages through the existing `plate_binding.dart` / `PlateView` path,
unchanged. This prompt restyles *everything around* the plate and touches the plate
itself only to set the surface it sits on.

## Do this

- Restyle `country_shelf.dart`, `plate_tile.dart` and `section_header.dart` in
  place. Do not create parallel widgets and leave the old ones wired.
- The plate's backing surface inside a card: read what the design uses and match
  it. Our plates are light objects on a dark card — make sure the card's fill
  does not fight the plate's own edge. If a plate needs a neutral plinth behind it,
  use `n900`, not white.
- Cards go to one column under `NBreak.detail` (1040) and the group grid collapses
  to a single column, per the design's media query. Section padding drops to
  `56px 40px 72px`.
- `All plates →` routes to the existing country detail screen (sk12 restyles it).

## Do not

- Do not change any plate geometry, spec, livery or country block.
- Do not add plate artwork from the design.
- Do not replace `GridView` with a package. `SliverGrid` /
  `SliverGridDelegateWithMaxCrossAxisExtent` is already in use and is fine.
- Do not hardcode entry/spec counts — derive them per country from the catalogue.
